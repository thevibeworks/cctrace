## Best choice: `≡`

```text
≡ cache@04:37
cache≡04:37
≡04:37
```

Why it works:

- **Slim:** three lightweight horizontal layers.
- **Terminal-safe:** generally renders as a single monospace cell.
- **Font-compatible:** widely supported across terminal fonts.
- **Visually balanced:** less heavy than boxed or filled symbols.
- **Conceptually close:** suggests stacked cache layers, memory tiers, or repeated stored entries.
- **Quiet:** noticeable without competing with progress bars or model indicators.
